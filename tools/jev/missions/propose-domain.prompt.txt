# HOW TO READ THE EVIDENCE

Variance between attempts is the signal. Where every attempt did the same
thing, the harness already determines behaviour. Where they diverged, the
harness left a decision to chance; those points are what to fix. Every
edit must trace to a moment in a trajectory or to a document in the
brief, and you cite it in your notes. If you cannot name the moment, the
edit is speculation and costs context for nothing.
