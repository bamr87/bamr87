The `/schedules` page reads the fleet's crons and the throughput caps from `_data/harness_registry.yml`. An hour is a collision only when more AI crons than the cap can fire in it on one day. The banner names the hour, the weekdays, and the exact minute the crons share, never the top of the hour.

Every step passed at both viewports: the route answered 200, the tiles, the chart and the schedule table rendered, the section says how an hour is marked, and the page did not scroll sideways. On the data of the run date the page shows one collision where it used to show three: the 06 UTC hour, 13 AI crons on Monday, 12 of them at exactly 06:17. The 14 and 15 UTC bars are no longer marked, because their crons fire on different weekdays.

The build read a snapshot regraded offline with this change's generator (`tools/dash harnesses --offline`), which is what the next daily pulse writes. The committed `_data/harness_registry.yml` is left to that pulse, so until it runs the published page shows the old three entries in the new wording.
