### Added

- **Stargazer, the open-source radar** (`tools/dash stars`, docs/STARGAZER.md): the account's GitHub stars sorted and categorised, the repos rising this week, this month and in the fleet's topics, and the repos most like each fleet project, with star velocity, star-farming flags and a review list per project. It renders on the console's new **Stars** page (`/stars`, `/api/stars`, also on the published site) and through the MCP's `fleet_stars`, and is refreshed daily by `stargazer.yml` (off until `vars.STARGAZER_ENABLED`). A SessionStart hook starts every local Claude Code session with the starred repos relevant to its project; `tools/dash stars hook --install --yes` extends that to sessions outside the hub.
- **AI reviews and themes for the radar** (`tools/dash stars review --yes`, the console's **Review with AI**): capped, tool-less Claude sessions through `dash-gen ai-run` give each repo a summary, a verdict (adopt · borrow · watch · skip), the fleet project it serves and ideas, and group the stars into themes (`_data/stars_ai.json`, laid over every build; agent `local:stars-review`). Shown on the Stars page's new **Themes** tab, in every table and the drawer, and in the session brief.
- **The radar in Grafana and as a map**: `tools/dash stars metrics --push` sends it to the OTel collector as gauges, and the rendered `fleet-stars` dashboard draws totals, categories, languages, stars over time, the fastest risers, the closest repos to the fleet and the AI verdicts; the Stars page's new **Map** tab plots every repo by similarity to the fleet against stars.
