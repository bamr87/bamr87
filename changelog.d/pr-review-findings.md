### Added

- `pr-review.yml`: the findings channel (constitution § 19.3). A repo check uploads a `fleet-findings` artifact of JSON lines (`path`, `line`, `rule`, `severity`, `message`, `kind`) instead of commenting itself, and the reusable posts each finding on a line the PR adds as one inline comment with its severity, a hidden `(path, line, rule)` key so a later push never reposts it, and the § 19.4 signature. `content` and `docs` findings are dropped on Dependabot and release-please PRs (§ 19.6), a malformed artifact posts nothing and still leaves the CI-failure sticky, at most 20 new comments post per review (and posting stops after GitHub refuses 5 in a row), the sticky counts findings by severity while something fails, and the job summary lists every finding and what happened to it. The caller's permissions are unchanged (`actions: read`, `pull-requests: write`); fleet stubs get it with the next `v2` minor cut. Contract: `templates/pr-review/README.md`.
